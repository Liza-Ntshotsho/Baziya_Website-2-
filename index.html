🌿 Welcome to Baziya Community Health Centre

Providing trusted primary healthcare services to families in Baziya Mission, Mthatha, and surrounding rural communities.

🏥 About Baziya Clinic

Baziya Clinic, formerly Baziya C.H.C, is a state-owned community health centre in the Eastern Cape, offering comprehensive healthcare to rural communities. Our mission is to provide accessible, integrated, and high-quality health services using all available resources efficiently.

Vision

A customer-driven health district, committed and capable of providing high-quality service standards for a better life for all communities.

Mission

To ensure accessible integrated district health services in the OR Tambo district using all resources to the optimum.

Values

  • Equity of distribution and quality services
  • Service excellence and patient satisfaction
  • Fair labour practices
  • Performance-driven organisation
  • Transparency

💚 Our Healthcare Services

  • General Consultations: Diagnosis and treatment for common illnesses.
  • Maternal & Child Health: Pregnancy support, delivery attendance, immunizations, and child growth monitoring.
  • HIV & STI Management: Counselling, testing, and treatment.
  • Family Planning: Consultations and contraceptive options.
  • Chronic Disease Care: Diabetes, hypertension, and other chronic conditions.
  • Pharmaceutical Services: Dispensing of prescribed medication.
  • Support Services: Access to dieticians, physiotherapy, and social work through referrals.

📅 Weekly Clinic Schedule

  • Monday: Child Immunization & Growth Monitoring
  • Tuesday: HIV Counselling & Testing
  • Wednesday: Antenatal Care & Family Planning
  • Thursday: Chronic Disease Management
  • Friday: General Consultations
  • Saturday: Emergency & Weekend Care
  • Sunday: Emergency Only

Note: Patients are advised to book in advance for specialized services.
